## A week to change fate

A strange program appears on your computer. The person on the other side of the screen claims to be the Grim Reaper—and says your soul is due. You have seven days to talk, choose, and decide what this unexpected connection will become.

*A Date with Death* turns an intimate chat window into a supernatural romance story. Its atmosphere is shaped by late-night messages, uneasy tenderness, and choices that make every reply feel personal.

## What makes it memorable

- **Conversation-led storytelling** — follow a relationship through messages, calls, and small moments of hesitation.
- **Choices that change the mood** — your tone and answers guide the direction of the story.
- **A character that feels like yours** — customize your protagonist’s identity and appearance.
- **A room of your own** — create a space that reflects the final week of your story.
- **Reasons to return** — revisit the story to explore different moments and endings.
